Managament is the act of getting people together to accomplish desired goals and objective using available resources efficiently and effectively. 
Management comprises planning, organizing, staffing, leading or directing and controlling an organization.

* Management exsits due to Business. The human needs leads to a business and the management team is built to handle this business operations. 
  Business is the exachange of good and services for value which is either paid by customers or for them by the government or through charities and
  are exchanged in terms of money and/or goods and services.

* Business not only impacts the company but it leads to overall development of the country by providing services to the people of the country and also social
services which makes life more affordable. 

* There are uncertainties in business due to competition, not adapting to the change. The business can only be forecasted but its never assured
of it's outcome. 

* Business will always try to minimize the known uncertainties and risks. It does so by keeping to minimum the scale of operation. Business will always try
  to reduce impact of business overhead by getting it compensated through **hard and smart** work.

* Either an individual or a group must play a role of an owner in any business. The one who is pioneer in a business is called **Enterpreneur Businessman**
  and the others who follow the same idea **Businessman**.

* Mangament and adminstrator are different concepts where Manager deals with the totality of the bueiness where as tha admin deals with the unit of operation.

* The twin tests of business, namely liquidity and profitability forces management to manage in such.a way that these objectives are achieved.


Some Keywords to note:

* **Privatization** : When services are provided by various for-profit companies at a price to the customer.
* **Liberalization**: Liberalization refers to the "removal of controls" to encourage economic development.
* **Globalization** : Globalization refers to free and unrestricted flow of products and services all over the world.
* **Liquidity**     : The ease and swiftness with which an asset can be converted into cash without suffering a significant loss.
* **Ownership-Interest** : Refers to the extent of involvement and hence the interest of the owners in running of the business.
