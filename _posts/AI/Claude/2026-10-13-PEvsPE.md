title: "Prompt Engineering vs Prompt Evaluation"
topic: AI > Claude
summary: "PE Vs PE"
---

Prompt Engineering is the art of writing prompts. Prompt Evaluation is the art of measuring how well your prompts work. 

Steps to ensure that the prompts are good for Production ready:

1. Testing the prompt once and decide it's good enough.
2. Testing the prompt few times and tweaking it accordingly to produce the desired outcome. (Edge cases).
3. Testing the prompt using an evaluation pipeline to score it and then iterating the prompt.

Recommended approach is to evaluate the prompt using pipeline to be production ready which ensures that all the scenarios are handled, iterate over 
measurable improvements.
